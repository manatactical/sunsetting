Your screens, softer at sundown.

A free menu-bar app that dims, warms and schedules every display on your Mac.

Everything your displays need. Nothing they don't.

  • Brightness per screen

    Set the built-in display and external monitors together, or one at a time. External screens are controlled over DDC/CI.

  • Limits per screen

    Give each display its own minimum and maximum, so every screen stays within the range you want.

  • Night Mode

    One switch caps every display at its minimum brightness.

  • Warmth

    Shift colour toward amber, from 6500K down to 3000K. Brightness stays where you set it.

  • Warm up over time

    Ramp warmth between two times of day, hold it overnight, and return to normal by morning.

  • Brightness keys

    F1 and F2 step all displays together, with the native macOS brightness overlay.

  • Find a screen

    Hover a row to outline that physical display. Right-click to give it a name.

  • Open at Login

    Start Sunsetting automatically when you log in.
